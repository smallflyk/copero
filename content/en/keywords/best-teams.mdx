## What are the best Pokémon Champions teams?

The best-supported Regulation M-B structures in the collected material include Mega Charizard Y balance, Mega Floette “Big Six,” Mega Aerodactyl flexibility, rain with Mega Swampert, and several setup or weather alternatives. Their common feature is role coverage: a Mega plan, speed control, damage, defensive positioning, and a late-game route. No single roster is best into every matchup.

The tournament source records complete teams and match results, while the official strategy article explains why the pieces work. A record belongs to one player in one event and should not be treated as the team’s universal win rate. Item choices also matter because Regulation M-B forbids duplicate held items.

## Mega Floette and Charizard “Big Six”

One documented six-Pokémon roster uses Charizard with Charizardite Y, Whimsicott with Focus Sash, Basculegion with Life Orb, Floette with Floettite, Garchomp with Roseli Berry, and Incineroar with Sitrus Berry. The supplied tournament snapshot records an 11-1 result for this exact roster at an August 12, 2026 online event. All six held items are different, matching the M-B item rule.

The official overview says this Charizard/Whimsicott/Floette structure became known as the “Big Six” and took five of the Top 8 places at Japan Nationals, including first. Whimsicott supplies Tailwind, Charizard supplies sun and spread Fire pressure, Floette adds Fairy damage, and Incineroar supports positioning. Garchomp and Basculegion provide distinct damage routes rather than duplicating the same attack profile.

The official M-B article describes a Charizard structure with Mega Aerodactyl as a second possible Mega. A successful version also uses Farigiraf for access to both Tailwind and Trick Room modes while Armor Tail blocks priority.

Focus Sash Kingambit and Ice Fang Mega Aerodactyl are specifically identified in the official recap of the NAIC runner-up variation. Garchomp remains a key Charizard partner because its Earthquake misses the Flying-type Charizard. ## Mega Swampert rain with a sun option

Another documented roster uses Swampert with Swampertite, Pelipper with Sitrus Berry, Archaludon with Leftovers, Grimmsnarl with Light Clay, Charizard with Charizardite Y, and Venusaur with Focus Sash. The source records a 12-2 result at an August 3, 2026 event. It contains both rain and sun pieces, allowing the player to choose a field plan rather than forcing all six into one weather mode.

The official overview explains the rain interactions: Mega Swampert gains speed through Swift Swim, Archaludon can charge Electro Shot immediately in rain, and Pelipper supplies Drizzle. Charizard and Venusaur create an alternate sun route. Because both weather setters can overwrite the field, piloting order matters more than simply selecting all weather Pokémon together.

The collected best-team data includes Garchomp with Choice Scarf, Sinistcha with Kasib Berry, Maushold with Focus Sash, Delphox with Delphoxite, Floette with Floettite, and Incineroar with Passho Berry. The official M-B analysis describes Mega Delphox around Nasty Plot with Incineroar and Sinistcha. Sinistcha’s Hospitality supports damaged partners, while Incineroar uses Intimidate and Parting Shot to create setup space. The official article also mentions Kingambit and Sneasler on common Delphox cores, showing that the exact six can change while the setup principle remains.

## Mega Froslass and Mega Scovillain roster

A documented roster uses Dusk Form Lycanroc with Focus Sash, Sneasler with White Herb, Froslass with Froslassite, Kingambit with Black Glasses, Scovillain with Scovillainite, and Basculegion with Sitrus Berry. The tournament snapshot records a 12-0 result at a July 29 event and shows broader aggregate results for teams of that composition. The official overview explains the two Mega roles. Mega Froslass sets snow with Snow Warning and can use Aurora Veil or accurate Blizzard, while Mega Scovillain spreads burns through Spicy Spray and can redirect attacks with Rage Powder. Lycanroc helps address Charizard pressure, and the roster can select the Mega that better fits the opposing six.

## How to read tournament records

A 13-1 or 12-0 record is evidence that a roster worked for a player in a particular event. It is not the same as a randomized trial proving the team will win at that rate for everyone. Player skill, pairings, best-of format, season timing, and field composition affect the result.

The source also provides aggregate match and team counts for some structures, but those figures belong to its selected dataset and date. Do not combine a single-event record, an aggregate composition percentage, and an official event placement as if they were one statistic. This page keeps roster evidence and strategy explanation separate for that reason.

## What the best teams have in common

Multiple Mega candidates appear repeatedly, but only one Mega Evolution is used per battle. Tailwind from Whimsicott, Trick Room access through Farigiraf, weather from Charizard or Pelipper, Fake Out from Incineroar or Sneasler, and priority from Kingambit create ways to control tempo. Strong rosters also preserve a late-game cleaner such as Basculegion.

Held items are distributed without duplication to satisfy M-B rules. Focus Sash protects a fragile utility slot, recovery berries or Leftovers support longer games, and offensive items give a chosen attacker specific knockout pressure. The exact item should be copied only with the exact roster and regulation it came from, not assigned by name recognition alone.

## Choosing a team to copy

Start with the field plan you understand. Choose Charizard/Whimsicott for direct Tailwind and sun, Swampert/Pelipper for rain, Delphox for protected setup, or Froslass/Scovillain for weather and redirection flexibility. Then verify that every species, form, Mega Stone, and held item is currently obtainable and legal.

Practice the bring-four decision instead of assuming the same four appear every match. Identify the opposing weather setter, speed control, priority blocker, and likely Mega during the 90-second preview. A proven six is a starting structure; learning when not to bring one member is part of using it correctly.
