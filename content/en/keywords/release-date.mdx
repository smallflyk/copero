## What is the Pokémon Champions release date?

The Pokémon Champions release date is April 8, 2026 for Nintendo Switch. The iOS and Android versions released on June 17, 2026. These dates are stated in the official Pokémon news entry collected for this project, which lists the genre as Battle and the platforms as Nintendo Switch, iOS, and Android.

The staggered launch explains why some early material describes the game as Switch-only or says mobile is coming later. Those statements reflected the period between April 8 and June 17 and are now outdated. Nintendo Switch 2 players are also mentioned in the official announcement and cross-platform copy, although the platform list groups the console release under Nintendo Switch.

## Nintendo Switch launch

Pokémon Champions first became available on Nintendo Switch in April 2026. The official June announcement says Trainers had been battling on Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2 since the April launch. The product is described as free-to-start rather than a traditional one-price retail release.

The official game site also references a Pokémon Champions + Starter Pack bundle and optional in-game items. The collected material does not provide a permanent price table or promise identical offers in every region. Current eShop pricing, download size, bundle contents, and Switch 2-specific performance are **Pending confirmation** in the live store.

## iOS and Android launch

Pokémon Champions came to the App Store and Google Play on June 17, 2026. Preregistration was available before release, according to the June 3 announcement. The mobile launch extended the same battle-focused game to phones and tablets rather than introducing a separately named mobile edition.

The Google Play snapshot describes Ranked, Casual, and Private Battles, plus access to Pokémon within the game and support for certain partners through Pokémon HOME. Exact iPhone, iPad, and Android device requirements are not included in the supplied research. Those compatibility details are **Pending confirmation** and should be checked on the current store listing.

## Save-data carryover at mobile launch

Existing Switch players were told that save data would carry over to mobile when the same Nintendo Account was linked. This is an official condition, not a general promise that any two accounts can be merged. Check the displayed account before linking, especially if multiple family members use the same console.

Cross-save and cross-platform play should not be confused. Save carryover continues one player’s progress across supported devices, while cross-platform play allows mobile users to battle Switch and Switch 2 users. The supplied material does not explain guest-account conversion, unlinking, or recovery after linking the wrong account, so those procedures remain **Pending confirmation**.

## Mobile launch gift dates

The mobile launch celebration offered Raichu, Raichunite X, and Raichunite Y through the in-game mailbox. The official claim window ran from Wednesday, June 17, 2026 through Tuesday, September 1, 2026 at 6:49 p.m. PDT. Both Switch and mobile players were included in the announcement.

This reward should not be confused with a serial code. The announcement says to claim it from the in-game mailbox, while typed codes use the Mystery Gifts menu. Mega Raichu X was announced with Electric Surge and Mega Raichu Y with No Guard, but future availability after the launch window is **Pending confirmation**.

## Platforms available at release

The official platform list names Nintendo Switch, iOS, and Android. The official site also says mobile users can battle Trainers playing on Nintendo Switch or Nintendo Switch 2. No native Windows, macOS, PlayStation, or Xbox release appears in the supplied official materials.

That absence is not proof that another platform can never be added. It means only that a PC or other console release is not confirmed by the research provided for this page. Any future platform date should remain **Pending confirmation** until it appears through an official Pokémon or platform-store announcement.

## What launched with the game?

The official overview defines Pokémon Champions as a dedicated battle game with familiar types, Abilities, and moves. Players can recruit Pokémon within the game, use certain past partners through Pokémon HOME, earn rewards through challenges, and customize their Trainer. Ranked, Casual, and Private Battles are documented on the mobile listing.

The sources do not present a traditional exploration story as the launch focus. They also do not specify a fixed end date for the service, a complete forever-roster, or a guarantee that every competitive rule will stay the same. Regulation windows, ranked seasons, available gifts, and store offers should all be treated as changeable live-game information.

## Release date versus update date

The Google Play snapshot says the Android listing was updated July 30, 2026. That date indicates a store update and does not replace the June 17 mobile release date. Later patches, seasons, events, and Battle Pass changes should also keep their own labels instead of being presented as new launches.

The same distinction applies to regulation dates. Regulation M-B began June 17 alongside the mobile release period, but it is a competitive rules window rather than a platform launch. Clear labels prevent an event start, app update, or gift deadline from being mistaken for the Pokémon Champions release date.

## Why different dates appear online

April 8 and June 17 are both correct, but they refer to different platforms. April 8 is the game’s Nintendo Switch release date, while June 17 is the iOS and Android release date. A page that uses only one date without a platform label can therefore be misleading.

Time zones also matter for limited distributions, not for the high-level global launch dates recorded here. The Raichu gift, for example, used a specific 6:49 p.m. PDT end time. For any event or code, use the date, time, and zone from the current in-game notice rather than assuming it ends at local midnight.

## Release timeline at a glance

The confirmed sequence is: Nintendo Switch launch on April 8, official mobile-date announcement on June 3, then iOS and Android launch on June 17. The mobile launch also began the Raichu and Mega Stone mailbox distribution. The Google Play research snapshot later showed a July 30, 2026 listing update, but an app-update date is not a new game release date.

As of the supplied research, Pokémon Champions is already available rather than upcoming. Store rollout in a particular country, device compatibility, and any future native PC release remain **Pending confirmation**. Use the official store for your platform as the final availability check.
