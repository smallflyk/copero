## Which Pokémon are in Pokémon Champions?

Pokémon Champions does not include every Pokémon. The collected roster source explicitly describes the game as battle-focused and says a set number of Pokémon are available. Players can recruit species inside the game, rent a species with Victory Points after obtaining it through the Recruit Ranch, receive some Pokémon as gifts, and connect Pokémon HOME for certain supported partners.

The supplied list spans Kanto through Paldea and includes regional forms and Mega Evolutions as separate battle entries. It begins with Venusaur and continues through Hydrapple in National Pokédex order. Because Champions is a live game, this page does not turn that snapshot into a permanent roster count.

## Kanto Pokémon in the collected roster

The Kanto portion includes Venusaur, Charizard, Blastoise, Beedrill, Pidgeot, Arbok, Pikachu, Raichu, Clefable, Ninetales, Vileplume, Arcanine, Alakazam, Machamp, Victreebel, Slowbro, Gengar, Kangaskhan, Starmie, Pinsir, Tauros, Gyarados, Ditto, Vaporeon, Jolteon, Flareon, Aerodactyl, Snorlax, and Dragonite. Alolan Raichu and regional variants of familiar species appear alongside their standard forms in the source. Duplicate species names in the raw list represent alternate forms, not accidental extra species.

Documented Kanto Mega forms include Mega Venusaur, both Mega Charizard X and Y, Mega Blastoise, Mega Beedrill, Mega Pidgeot, both Mega Raichu forms, Mega Clefable, Mega Alakazam, Mega Victreebel, Mega Slowbro, Mega Gengar, Mega Kangaskhan, Mega Starmie, Mega Pinsir, Mega Gyarados, Mega Aerodactyl, and Mega Dragonite. A Mega form is not a second base species, and Regulation M-B still limits a player to one Mega Evolution per battle. The required Mega Stone must also be available to the account.

## Johto Pokémon in the collected roster

Johto entries include Meganium, Typhlosion, Feraligatr, Ariados, Ampharos, Azumarill, Politoed, Espeon, Umbreon, Slowking, Forretress, Steelix, Qwilfish, Scizor, Heracross, Skarmory, Houndoom, and Tyranitar. The list also shows regional versions where applicable, including Hisuian Typhlosion and Galarian Slowking. Form-specific type combinations should be checked on the roster screen.

Mega Meganium, Mega Feraligatr, Mega Ampharos, Mega Steelix, Mega Scizor, Mega Heracross, Mega Skarmory, Mega Houndoom, and Mega Tyranitar are documented. Their presence in the available roster does not mean every Mega is legal in every ranked regulation. Always check the current rules before building around a Mega Stone.

## Hoenn Pokémon in the collected roster

These entries support several weather and balance structures described in the M-B strategy material. Pelipper and Swampert, for example, appear together in documented rain teams.

The roster includes Mega forms for Sceptile, Blaziken, Swampert, Gardevoir, Sableye, Mawile, Aggron, Medicham, Manectric, Sharpedo, Camerupt, Altaria, Banette, Chimecho, Absol, Glalie, and Metagross. A form’s item, Ability, or tournament role should be checked on its current detail screen rather than inferred from another Pokémon game. Roster presence alone does not document a complete build.

## How to get Pokémon for your team

The official game overview says players can recruit Pokémon within Pokémon Champions and bring certain past partners through Pokémon HOME. The roster source adds that species rentals use Victory Points after Recruit Ranch acquisition and that some Pokémon arrive as gifts. It does not give one universal acquisition step for every species.

Do not assume a Pokémon HOME transfer bypasses the supported roster. “Certain Pokémon” means compatibility is selective, and current regulation legality creates another filter after transfer. If a desired form, move, or individual is rejected, the exact reason is **Pending confirmation** until the game’s message or official support explains it.

## Available does not mean legal in Ranked

The available Pokémon list answers which species and forms the game snapshot recognizes. Regulation M-B answers which entries may be registered under that ruleset, and its separate Mega section answers which Mega Evolutions are legal. A Pokémon can therefore be available for collection or another mode without being usable in the current ladder.

Before spending Victory Points or planning a transfer, verify three things: the species is still in the live roster, the desired form is supported, and the active regulation accepts it. Then check held-item duplication and the one-Mega-per-battle rule. This prevents a valid collection choice from becoming an unusable ranked team slot.
