## Is Pokémon Champions on mobile?

Yes. Pokémon Champions mobile launched for iOS and Android on June 17, 2026. The official announcement links the App Store and Google Play versions and lists Nintendo Switch, iOS, and Android as the game’s platforms. It is a free-to-start release, with optional purchases described separately by the official site.

The mobile version is the same battle-focused Pokémon Champions experience described on the official game page. Players can use Ranked Battles for global competition, Casual Battles for a less formal match, and Private Battles with family, friends, or other Trainers. A separate mobile-only ruleset is not documented in the supplied material.

## Mobile release date and launch window

The mobile release date was June 17, 2026. Pokémon Champions had already launched on Nintendo Switch in April, so the iOS and Android versions arrived later. The source announcement was published June 3 and stated that preregistration was open before launch.

A launch celebration made Raichu, Raichunite X, and Raichunite Y available through the in-game mailbox from June 17 through September 1, 2026 at 6:49 p.m. PDT. This was a mailbox distribution, not one of the typed Mystery Gift codes listed on the codes page. The offer is date-limited, so its availability after that deadline is not implied.

## Cross-platform play on iOS and Android

Mobile players can battle against Trainers using Nintendo Switch or Nintendo Switch 2. This cross-platform support is stated directly on the official Pokémon Champions site. The materials do not describe a switch that limits matchmaking to mobile-only opponents, so that option is **待确认**.

Cross-platform matchmaking does not erase regulation rules. Ranked teams still need to satisfy the active format, and a roster available in the game may not be legal in every competitive window. Before entering Ranked, check the current in-game notice for eligible Pokémon, Mega Evolution options, held-item limits, and timers.

## Carrying Switch save data to mobile

Players coming from Nintendo Switch can carry their save data to the mobile version by linking the same Nintendo Account. That condition is explicit in the official mobile announcement. It is safer to confirm the account identity on both devices before linking because the material does not promise transfers between different Nintendo Accounts.

Save carryover and cross-platform play are different features. Carryover lets one player continue progress on another supported device, while cross-platform play lets different devices meet in battle. The research does not document guest-account migration, account merging, or recovery after an incorrect link, so those cases are **待确认**.

## Pokémon HOME support on mobile

The Google Play description says players can link Pokémon HOME and team up with certain Pokémon they previously befriended. It also says players without earlier Pokémon-series experience can find Pokémon to battle alongside within Pokémon Champions. This makes HOME an additional roster path rather than the only way to build a team.

“Certain Pokémon” is the controlling phrase. The collected material does not claim that every Pokémon stored in HOME can enter Champions, and the available roster source explicitly says not all species are present. Players should verify species support and current regulation legality before planning around a transfer.
